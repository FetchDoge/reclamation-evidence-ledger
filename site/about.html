About Reclamation Evidence

Calgary, Alberta · Founded by Marcus Richards

The Core Thesis

Field capacity in Western Canadian closure programs is strictly finite. Environmental coordinators, agrologists, and field technicians have limited summer road days to visit hundreds of orphaned, suspended, and reclaimed wellsites across Alberta and Saskatchewan.

Screening earns field days. By calculating month-matched vegetation trajectories over 5 years of Sentinel-2 satellite passes, we triage disturbed leases before the snow melts. Field crews go first to the sites that need eyes, rather than driving blindly between locations that are already thriving or stalled.

Provenance-First Engineering

Every claim we publish carries its complete mathematical method, its satellite imagery timestamps, and its misses. We believe trust in environmental screening cannot be bought with corporate buzzwords or opaque black-box machine learning. Real authority comes from stated limits.
