Methodology, Triad & Provenance

Pre-registered Protocol

Reclamation Evidence is a screening service for reclamation and closure programs in the Western Canadian Sedimentary Basin. We rank disturbed sites by vegetation recovery signal from Sentinel-2 satellite imagery so field crews go first to the sites that need eyes.

1. Detection

Vegetation recovery signal above the ±0.08 noise threshold from month-matched Sentinel-2 imagery.

2. Identification

Distinguishing true vegetation trend from seasonal phenology artifacts and regional drought noise.

3. Attribution (Refused)

We never attribute causation, contamination status, or liability from orbit. That requires qualified field professionals.

The Self-Audit Story (Our Credibility Core)

Our first method called all 27 pilot sites "recovering." That was a phenology artifact caused by mismatched seasonal sampling. We caught it, printed the correction, rebuilt the method month-matched, and published the whole audit history publicly. Confidence comes from stated limits.

What We Don't Do · Stated Operating Boundaries
No compliance or reclamation certification: We never certify reclamation, issue closure certificates, or replace regulatory filings.
No contamination status: No contamination status, causation, responsibility, or attribution.
No replacement for field assessment: Orbit provides screening triage only; qualified professional boots on the ground are always required.
