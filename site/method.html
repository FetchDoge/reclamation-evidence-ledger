Month-Matched Sentinel-2 Screening Architecture

Standard NDVI changes are notoriously vulnerable to false positives. Comparing an August satellite capture against a June baseline produces artificial "recovery" spikes simply because prairie crops and grasses peak later in the season.

Month-Matched Median Formula:
Delta = Median[ NDVI_current(m) - NDVI_baseline(m) ] for matched months m ∈ {May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep}
Thresholds: Delta > +0.08 → Recovering | Delta < -0.08 → Stalled / Regressing | Inside [-0.08, +0.08] → Noise band

Uncertainty Quantification & Honest Missing Intervals

Every delta calculation is backed by a 95% bootstrap confidence interval over matched-month deltas. We do not hide gaps: in our 99-site ledger, exactly 12 sites carry no confidence interval due to cloud-cover constraints during specific baseline passes. We document this candidly in the register.

The Self-Audit Story in Full

When we tested our initial v1 screening protocol across 27 pilot sites, our algorithm reported that all 27 were "recovering." That result was too neat. Closer inspection revealed a severe phenology artifact: dry spring baselines compared against wet mid-summer observations. Rather than conceal the flaw, we retracted the run, rebuilt our ingestion pipeline around month-matching, validated synthetic test traces (+0.30, +0.01, -0.30) that correctly rejected the trap, and published our methodology along with our misses in the public ledger.
