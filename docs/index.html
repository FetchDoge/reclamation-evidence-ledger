PLATE I

The Instrument Audited Itself

The most important thing this project produced wasn’t a green dashboard — it was a caught mistake.

V1 · SEASONAL MEDIANS

All 27 sites “recovering”

The first change-detection method compared seasonal vegetation medians and reported that every pilot site was recovering. A green dashboard. It was wrong.

AUDIT · 2026-09-27

Measuring the calendar

A full audit found the flaw: baseline scenes were sampled in May/June while current scenes were sampled in July. Summer is greener than spring — the method was measuring phenology, not reclamation.

V2 · MONTH-MATCHED

Honest refusals

Rebuilt around month-matched median-of-deltas: each calendar month compared only to itself, ≥2 matched months required, honest refusal otherwise. Under the corrected method most sites show no significant change — and the ledger says so.

PLATE IV

Method — Why Month-Matching

Greenness follows the calendar, not the project. So the ledger never compares July to May.

Fig. 6 — The month-matched comparison (the v1 flaw, and the fix)
Pipeline change.py, month-matched median-of-deltas; the v1 seasonal-median flaw and its fix are documented in Plate I.
Doctrine — borrowed from imagery interpretation tradecraft

Detection

Something changed vs the baseline — a measured delta. The pipeline’s lowest claim, and an honest one.

Identification

Withheld (Oct 2026). Would mean NDVI in the analysis square moved beyond ±0.08 with a 95% CI excluding zero — a screening hypothesis about the square, never a reclamation finding. Not published until a surrounding-land baseline passes a no-well control test.

Attribution

A named party is responsible. Never emitted by code. Requires human review and ground truth. The pipeline stops at identified.

Honest limits
  • Sentinel-2’s 10 m pixels cannot resolve wellheads or prove contamination, legal compliance, causation, or methane emissions. This is not a methane detection system.
  • The 500 m buffer is a ~1 km² square, mostly land around the pad. In 4 of 5 hand-checked pilot sites no pad was visible at 10 m.
  • Growing season to growing season only (May–Sep). Alberta snow makes winter comparisons meaningless; drought years mimic non-recovery.
  • Site coordinates are DLS-derived LSD centroids. Their accuracy was withdrawn on 2026-10-09: a published diagnosis measured a median error of 2,658 m (NEGATIVE-RESULT-2026-10-09.md). Do not screen on these positions. This is not a survey of the wellhead.
  • No interval below four matched months. Bootstrap confidence intervals over matched-month deltas are degenerate at n=2 — the resampled-median distribution has only three distinct values, so the interval looks precise and means almost nothing. Below four matched months the ledger prints no interval rather than a misleading one.
  • Every public claim carries its claim tier and provenance. No exceptions.
PLATE V

The Ledger Keeps Auditing Itself

Plate V figures are typed from the 2026-09-27 BACI probe notes, not computed at render time. No control correction is in the pipeline yet.

After the rebuild, the instrument was stress-tested with a before-after-control-impact (BACI) probe: can a paired control parcel separate weather from reclamation? Twin-first — cheap simulation before any new downloads.

VERDICT · GO — WITH CHANGES

The probe reproduced the pipeline’s month-matched deltas bit-for-bit on four sites, then found five things. Three of them changed the pipeline.

F1 · GEOMETRY

In-chip controls are impossible

The chip is the 500 m assessment buffer — no room inside it for a true control. Real controls need fresh 1.2 km-offset chips.

F2 · HETEROGENEITY

Control selection is the whole game

Baseline NDVI spreads of 0.15–0.20 within one chip swing the result wildly. Proximity alone doesn’t make a valid control — land-cover matching does.

F3 · FIXED IN PIPELINE

Degenerate confidence intervals

At 2 matched months the bootstrap CI looks precise and means nothing. The probe’s fix is now a rule in change.py: no interval printed below 4 matched months.

F4 · ROBUSTNESS

Off-by-one chip dimensions handled

10 of 513 scenes have truncated window dimensions; the pipeline pins bands per-scene, so nothing breaks — verified, not assumed.

F5 · ECONOMY

Sites cluster; controls can be shared

26 of 27 pilot sites have a neighbour within 3 km. One control chip could serve two nearby sites — pending per-pair land-cover validation.

STANDING RULE

Probes before downloads

No download campaign runs until a cheap simulation has found what breaks. The BACI full run — 27 control chips, ~39 MB, under an hour — is greenlit once the land-cover pre-flight is implemented.
