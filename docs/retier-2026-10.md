# Re-tier diff, 2026-10

> **Re-scoring in progress (Oct 2026): site-level calls are withheld pending a surrounding-land baseline.** Every site is published as *detected – screening only*.

Run 2026-10-10 (MT) with `ops/retier_packets.py` at commit `4a15cda`. No imagery was re-fetched: every packet's delta, CI, medians and observation counts were recomputed from the observations stored in the packet and matched the stored values exactly before any claim changed.

## What changed and why

1. **The v1 tier rule ignored the confidence interval.** It called a site `identified` ("vegetation recovering" or "stalled or regressing") whenever |median NDVI delta| ≥ 0.08. The corrected screen rule (`ci-gated-v2`) is: identified only if |median matched-month NDVI delta| >= threshold AND a bootstrap 95% CI exists (>= 4 matched months) AND the CI excludes zero; otherwise detected. Of the 28 v1 `identified` sites, 18 fail it: 6 have no CI (fewer than 4 matched months) and 12 have a CI that includes zero.
2. **Site-level calls are withheld (2026-10-10).** Site-level 'identified' calls are withheld pending a surrounding-land (pad-vs-ring) baseline: no-well control points still produced 3/100 'identified' under the current rule (Seedling Lab no-well test, 2026-10-08). The corrected screen is still computed and kept in each packet (`claim.screen_tier_internal`) for internal tracking, but it is not published as a site-level call.

## What the screen does not show

- Each delta describes NDVI in the ~1 km² analysis square around a DLS-derived centroid (coordinate accuracy withdrawn 2026-10-09; see NEGATIVE-RESULT-2026-10-09.md), mostly land around the pad, not the pad.
- The CI resamples matched months from the same two baseline and two current years: within-season consistency, not year-to-year variability. The baseline includes the 2023 drought and 80 of 99 deltas are positive, which points to a regional signal.
- Screening only. Not legal proof, not a certification, not a methane, soil, contamination or subsurface measurement. Nothing here names or judges any operator.
