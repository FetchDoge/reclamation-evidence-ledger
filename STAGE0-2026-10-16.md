## 1. Verified geolocation (`ledger/geolocate.py`)
Real sources checked 2026-10-16:
- **AER ST37 "List of Wells in Alberta"** (free, monthly): `https://www.aer.ca/prd/documents/sts/st37/ST_37_Excel.zip`
  (HTTP 200 range request OK; 183,921,877 bytes, Last-Modified 2026-10-01 18:43 GMT). Surface-hole
  `SH_Actual_Latitude/Longitude` + `Licence_Surface_Location_Label` per well licence.
- **OWA inventory** has `License Number` and DLS site names, **no coordinates**.
- DLS LSD centroid (`ledger.sites.dls_to_latlon`): kept only as a recorded, withdrawn fallback; it never verifies a site.
- Not used (licence unconfirmed / not fetched): ABMI Human Footprint polygons, AER Well Licence List.
`resolve` joins OWA to ST37 by licence number, requires the ST37 location label to agree with the OWA
site name at section/township/range, cross-checks any extra source CSV (`--extra`, flagged if > 250 m
apart), and otherwise marks the site **`unverified`**. Uncertainty for ST37 is a *configured nominal
100 m* (rounded up from the largest point-to-footprint offset, 87 m, in Seedling Lab's 40-well HFI
check), not a measured per-site error. "Verified" means "authoritative coordinate, internally
consistent", not "pad resolved at 10 m".

Measured legacy-geocoder error vs ST37 (haversine):
| Set | n verified | median | 5-95% | min-max | within 300 m / 500 m |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 99 pilot sites | 99/99 | 2,405 m | 1,397-3,911 m | 1,165-4,378 m | 0 / 0 |
| All 22,057 OWA inventory sites (2026-10-01) | 21,340 | 2,712 m | 1,077-4,534 m | 118-5,705 m | 10 / 19 |
0 of 99 pilot analysis squares (+/-500 m) contain the ST37 well. 717 inventory sites stay unverified
(flags: 328 ST37 label disagrees with site name, 310 non-well component, 41 no licence, 31 licence not in
ST37, 7 bad coordinate). Files: `ops/results/geolocation-*`.

Gate: `ledger.change assess` now takes `--geolocation`. No verified record, chips not fetched within 50 m
of the verified point, or < 2 matched months => a schema-valid **`not_assessable`** packet. The schema
(1.2.0) also forbids any tier other than `not_assessable` when `site.location.status` is `unverified`.
`ledger.imagery fetch --geolocation` fetches only at verified coordinates.
